# RF Physics knowledge base — project instructions

This directory is a research knowledge base on RF, analog electronics, antennas, radar, phased
arrays, LoRa and related design (built for Marcus / SUTD Space Bar avionics).

- **Start every session by reading HANDOFF.md** — its top section "NEW SESSION? START HERE" says exactly what is paused and how to relaunch it.
- **Writing or editing KB pages:** follow CONVENTIONS.md exactly (page template, sourcing,
  verification, no copyrighted text, research-log protocol).
- **Answering questions:** look up INDEX.md / FAQ.md → read the relevant `kb/` pages → answer
  consistently with them at the user's level; cite the pages. If the KB is silent or
  low-confidence, say so, research, then extend the KB and record it in gaps/GAP_REGISTER.md.
- Parallel research agents: never more than 10 at once (raised from 6 by the user on 2026-09-27); each must keep a log in research-logs/.
