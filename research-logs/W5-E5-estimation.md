## Contradictions / discrepancies found
- SNR convention (complex-baseband MF SNR = E/N0 with power-preserving envelope; coherent real MF peak = 2E/N0) — 11/ambiguity agrees; 10/matched-filter note mixes scalings. Already tracked as GAP_REGISTER G-10-90 (D6 #8, owner W5-E9); flagged on 00/estimation page and 00/_sources, not edited.
- Wikipedia's α-filter formula uses a different tracking-index definition (step std σ_w T²/2) from its α-β formula — both verified with the right definitions (Kalman page "convention trap").
- α-β-γ third-gain conventions differ by 2 across texts (γ = β²/α with K3 = γ/2T²; γ' = β²/2α with K3 = γ'/T²).
- k_m normalisation: Sandia 1.62 per λ/D (uniform aperture, optimum squint) vs Gaussian-beam 1.44 per sum-beam width at 0.4 θ_b — not a contradiction, a normalisation difference; stated on the radar page.
- 05/data-converters says 1-bit ΣΔ sims fall 4–9 dB short of linear theory; my half-scale sims: 1st order 1.3 dB better, 2nd order 3.2 dB worse than the in-band-noise model — amplitude-dependent; reconciled in a sentence on the z-transform page.
- (original note) matched-filter page convention note says complex-baseband peak SNR Ẽ/N0 "equals" real-passband 2E/N0 with noise N0/2 per I and Q; ambiguity page says the complex-baseband SNR equals E/N0 of the real passband signal. These two statements use different down-conversion scalings. Recorded for W6 audit; my CRB page states results in terms of per-sample complex noise variance to avoid the ambiguity.
