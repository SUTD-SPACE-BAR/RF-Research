## Contradictions / discrepancies found
- (Coordinator request from W3-C4, 2026-09-29) u-blox altitude/velocity limits AND vs OR: NO primary u-blox document (datasheets S1, S3, S24; integration manuals S2, S4; interface description S5; M8 receiver description S25) states AND/OR logic — they list Dynamics ≤4 g, Altitude, Velocity 500 m/s as separate "operational limits (assuming Airborne 4 g)". Per-firmware: altitude 50 km (u-blox 7, M8, ZED-F9P-00B/01B HPG 1.00–1.12) → 80 km (ZED-F9P-02B HPG 1.13+, -04B HPG 1.32, M10 SPG 5.x; M10 migration note "maximum altitude limit increased to 80 000 m"). Only u-blox-staff statement found (S7, u-blox 7, 2016): no data above 500 m/s regardless of altitude. BigRedBee (S9) claims AND (1000 kn & 18 km) — vendor anecdote, contradicts S7.
