## Contradictions / discrepancies found
- D-RAP's fitted HAF line (10 log flux + 65) gives 22 MHz for M5 and 32 MHz for X5, whereas the SWPC table it was fitted to says 20 and 30 MHz (2 MHz fit residual) — quote both.
- Exam pool E3B01 says TEP occurs "between points separated by 2,000 to 3,000 miles" (3200–4800 km) and E3B02 "maximum ~5,000 miles" (8000 km); Australian SWS gives aTEP 5000–6500 km and eTEP 3000–8000 km. Pool numbers are in the right family but lower; state both.
- D-layer (non-deviative) absorption frequency law: existing ionospheric-hf-propagation page says ∝ 1/f²; SWPC D-RAP (citing Davies 1990, Stonehocker 1970, Sauer & Wilkinson 2008) uses the empirical (f0/f)^1.5; theory gives ∝ 1/(f ± f_L)² for f ≫ collision frequency; RESOLVED: P.533-14 (as coded in the ITU reference program) uses (f + fL)^−2 with fL = |fH sin I| at 100 km, not (f + fH)^−1.98. All three laws are now stated in space-weather Level 2 and the ionospheric page.
- ITURHFProp vs P.533 text: for D > dmax the code evaluates eq. (3) at d = D/n0 at the T + d0/2 and R − d0/2 control points, where the text says "F2(dmax)MUF". The code also evaluates the maps at UT = input hour but the solar geometry at hour − 1. Its printed elevations imply hr = 305.8 km for all modes, whereas my transcription of eqs 14–16 gives about 185 km (unresolved).
- P.368 ground constants: a search summary gives "very dry ground 0.1 mS/m, εr 3" vs the commonly quoted 0.3 mS/m, εr 7. Unresolved, so the category is omitted.
