## Contradictions / discrepancies found
- Internal (KB): dipoles-and-monopoles.md Level 3 said a thin 433 MHz dipole (λ/d ≈ 350) has Q ≈ 8–10 and VSWR-2 BW ≈ 8–10 % to 72 Ω; own MoM gives Q_Z = 5.9, BW 11.8 % (72 Ω) / 8.2 % (50 Ω); Q ≈ 9 needs λ/d ≈ 3500. Corrected in the page with a note.
- PCB slot pre-registration: expected dielectric to LOWER the centre-fed slot resistance; openEMS shows it RISES (488 → 714 Ω, infinite board) and then depends strongly on board size (423–613 Ω). Centre-fed R of a PCB slot is not a portable number.
- NXP AN11755 eq. (3) as extracted by pdftotext appears to give Rtr = (Rmatch/2)/den, but only Rtr = Rmatch/den (differential, consistent with the factor 2 in Xtr) makes eqs. (5)–(6) agree with an exact circuit solution. Not a contradiction in physics — a convention/reading issue; flagged in the page.
- Random-wire 'good lengths': VE3EED list vs Sprott's computed gaps use different criteria; NEC shows neither is robust to the counterpoise/ground (71 ft clean in one model, 4–10 kΩ points in two others).
- G5RV: Varney quotes VSWR ≈ 1.8 on 20 m with 50 Ω coax; NEC (10.4 m, average ground) gives 2.3–7.0 across 20 m.
- OCF at 1/3: NEC ≈ 80–140 Ω (2:1 balun) vs common 4:1/6:1 practice (vendor/forum).
