## Contradictions / discrepancies found
- Own-script bugs worth recording (not source contradictions): Gardner TED sign inversion locks on zero crossings; tight burst window truncates CRC; float64-cumsum CIC fails over long records — all documented in pages as Common mistakes.
- NCO SFDR 'rule' 6.02 dB per phase bit (widely quoted) is the typical/odd-FTW value; worst case for a conventional accumulator is 6.02P − 3.92 dB (Nicholas & Samueli 1987; reproduced numerically).
- Pluto sustained USB2 throughput: ADI wiki 'up to 4 MSPS no dropped samples'; RTL-SDR.com loss above 4 MSPS; PySDR ~5 MS/s; Libre Space Foundation eval 7.5–12 MSPS depending on host. Host-dependent; page quotes range.
- Airspy R2/Mini NF: vendor 3.5 dB vs measured 7.0–7.9 dB @435 MHz (Rowetel) and 7.1–10.7 dB (LSF eval, 'could not reproduce').
- RTL-SDR V4 vs V3: V4 claimed better filtering but '2–3 dB less sensitivity on some bands' (RTL-SDR Blog's own release note).
- harris (1978) Table 1 vs direct computation (N=1024, periodic windows): Hamming scalloping printed 1.78 dB, analytic 1.752 dB; Blackman 3 dB/6 dB BW printed 1.68/2.35 bins, computed 1.65/2.30; 4-term Blackman–Harris 6 dB BW printed 2.72, computed 2.67. All other entries agree to printed precision. Page quotes computed values and notes the difference.
- PySDR ch.3 lists RTL-SDR among direct-conversion (zero-IF) SDRs; the R820T/R820T2/R828D tuner is a low-IF tuner (IF ≈ 3.57 MHz) digitised by the RTL2832U, which does the final digital downconversion (E4000-based dongles were zero-IF). CONFIRMED (superkuh community doc; R820T datasheet via helper) and recorded in IQ page (Common mistakes 6) and NCO page.
