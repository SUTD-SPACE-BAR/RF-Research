- 2026-10-13 — G-11-01 source: TI SPYY007 (K. Ramasubramanian, "Using a complex-baseband architecture in FMCW radar systems", May 2017; read via pdftotext). Facts: echo is always delayed, so with an up-chirp the image band holds only noise; a real mixer folds image-band noise in-band (SNR loss "up to 3 dB"); a quadrature mixer gives DSB-vs-SSB NF advantage, theoretical 3 dB, less in practice (RX power split into I/Q, higher IF-noise share); full 3 dB when a TX noise skirt (antenna coupling/bumper) dominates; image band lets you monitor interference and the noise floor; the IM product 2f1−f2 of leakage + strong target falls in the image band (−f2) and folds in-band in real-only; data rate real 2fb,max vs complex fb,max (same); N-point complex FFT vs 2N-point real FFT, same memory. Table 1 "NF better than baseline by up to 3 dB". Plan: MC-verify the 3 dB and an IQ-imbalance IRR ghost in s9_fmcw_if_bistatic.py.
