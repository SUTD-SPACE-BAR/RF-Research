## 6. Misconceptions and knowledge gaps revealed

1. **"Radial acceleration = the motor's acceleration."** S17 added the v⊥²/R term in 15/09/20, but seven older pages still equate the two (#7). This is the only physics refinement in Wave 5 that changes a design decision (#3: SF9/125 at 868/915 MHz). *Gap:* no page gives the range-acceleration envelope as a function of station offset for other flight profiles. Only the 10 km reference flight is tabulated.
2. **Extrapolating a model past its validity.** P.372 man-made lines above 250 MHz (#4); ITM below 1 m (16, already flagged there). The KB states the rule on 07 and 15 but not on 08.
3. **Band choice for the 900 MHz radio.** Older pages assume 917–925 MHz as "the" Singapore 900 MHz band (#5, #14). The KB's settled advice is 866–869 MHz, with refarm and interpretation caveats. *Gap:* there is still no IMDA confirmation that rocket telemetry counts as "tracking, tracing and data acquisition" (sub-band 29). This is an open question for IMDA, not for research.
4. **Factor-of-2 in geometry, not only in dB:** trimming four edges vs one side (#12), and LDRO halving the payload bits per symbol (#9). Both are "which quantity" ambiguities of the same kind as the D6 3 dB traps.
5. **Process.** `build_index.py --check` passes cleanly, but it does not check anchors or front matter. This audit's anchor and front-matter checks found one or two problems the official check could not. Adding them is cheap.
