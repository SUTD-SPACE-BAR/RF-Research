=== 1. Why 32 768 Hz ===
2^15 = 32768 -> 15 divide-by-2 stages give exactly 1 Hz
AT-cut plate for 32.768 kHz would be 50.7 mm thick (vs ~0.05 mm for 32 MHz: 52 um)
-> 32 kHz uses a flexural tuning fork (f ~ w/L^2), a few mm long
